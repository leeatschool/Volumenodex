# Volumenodex v2.2.0 — Screenwriting Studio & Creative Suite Release

**Volumenodex** is a native standalone word processing studio crafted for creative storytellers, screenwriters, non-fiction authors, and academic researchers with physical print fidelity and deep narrative tools.

---

### 🎬 What's New in Version 2.2.0

#### 1. Modern Screenwriting Authoring Mode
- **Industry-Standard Screenplay Layout**: Dedicated manuscript mode formatted in Courier 12pt with authentic screenplay margins, element alignments, and pagination.
- **Smart Contextual Autoformatting**: Automatically detects and formats:
  - **Scene Headings** (`INT.`, `EXT.`, `INT./EXT.`) with uppercase standardization, location names, and time of day (`DAY`, `NIGHT`, `DUSK`, `CONTINUOUS`).
  - **Character Names** centered and capitalized above spoken dialogue.
  - **Dialogue & Parentheticals** indented to screenplay standards with seamless cursor progression.
  - **Transitions** (`CUT TO:`, `FADE OUT.`, `DISSOLVE TO:`) right-aligned automatically.
- **Smooth Element Cycling**: Tab and Enter navigation allows fast, uninterrupted scriptwriting by automatically advancing between Character, Dialogue, Parenthetical, Action, and Scene Heading states.
- **Dedicated Screenwriting Ribbon**: Quick-insert buttons, element pickers, and view options directly in the top ribbon toolbar.

#### 2. Screenplay Codex (Studio Companion)
- **Specialized Scriptwriting Companion**: A purpose-built counterpart to the Writer's Codex, tailored specifically for scripts and screenplays.
- **Beat Sheets & Act Structuring**: Built-in trackers for standard 3-Act structures, Save the Cat beat sheets, loglines, treatments, and sequence breakdowns.
- **Character & Cast Manager**: Track character arcs, recurring roles, scene appearances, and dialogue counts.
- **Real-Time Script Metrics**: Instant statistics on scene counts, estimated screen time (~1 page per minute), and dialogue-to-action ratios.

#### 3. Drag-and-Drop Screenplay Phrases Palette
- **Curated Categorized Terms**: Preloaded with the most common and standard script elements:
  - **Headings & Locations**: Exteriors (`EXT. HOME - NIGHT`, `EXT. CITY STREET - DAY`) & Interiors (`INT. APARTMENT - DAY`, `INT. POLICE STATION - NIGHT`).
  - **Lighting & Atmospheric Direction**: Prompts like `LOW LIGHTING, LIT BY STREET LIGHTS ALONE`, `HARSH OVERHEAD FLUORESCENT`, `GOLDEN HOUR SUNSET`, `PITCH BLACK / FLASHLIGHT BEAMS`.
  - **Stage & Prop Directions**: Common blocking directions like `BEAT`, `PAUSE`, `EXCHANGES LOOKS`, `REACHES FOR PROP`, `OFF-SCREEN / O.S.`.
  - **Act & Scene Dividers**: `ACT I: THE SETUP`, `INCITING INCIDENT`, `ACT II: THE CONFRONTATION`, `MIDPOINT`, `ACT III: RESOLUTION`.
- **Canvas Drag-and-Drop & Double-Click**: Drag any term directly from the side drawer and drop it straight onto the canvas page, or double-click to insert instantly at the cursor.
- **Custom Notes & Phrases**: Create, edit, categorize, and persist your own custom notes and recurring phrases for effortless reuse across projects.

#### 4. Redesigned Responsive New Document Dialog
- **Balanced 2x2 Grid Layout**: Replaced the tall vertical scrolling dialog with a compact, modern 2x2 cards layout (Novel & Prose, Academic & Research, Screenwriting Studio, Freeform Canvas).
- **All Modes Immediately Visible**: Eliminates vertical overflow or off-screen clipping, ensuring all 4 authoring modes are visible, clickable, and accessible on all monitor resolutions.

#### 5. Comprehensive Quality & Test Verification
- Passed all 119 unit and integration tests across all features, performance benchmarks, and document model integrity.
